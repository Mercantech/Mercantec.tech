# Bæver · Blå team

Original AI-genereret karakterillustration til Sikkerhed og test. Navnet Bæver er foreløbigt; filnavnet er stabilt ved senere navngivning.

- Fil: `portrait.png`
- Genereret: 7. november 2026 med den indbyggede `image_gen.imagegen` i standardtilstand, `transparent_background: true`.
- Ingen API-/CLI-fallback anvendt.
- Illustrationens stil er beskrevet ud fra den eksisterende Raymond-professor (`../raymond/professor.webp` fra denne filplacering); motivet er en ny, selvstændig bæver.
- Den originale genererede PNG er kopieret uændret og bevarer sin alfakanal. Ingen efterfølgende billedredigering.
- Kontrolleret: kvadratisk 1254 × 1254, RGBA med ægte transparens, fuld figur med synlige fødder og flad bæverhale, blå jakke og tablet med abstrakte loglinjer/testindikatorer.

## Præcis prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent full-body teaching-character portrait for an educational website, square composition. Create ONE entirely original anthropomorphic brown beaver who teaches blue-team security and software testing. He is a warm, competent adult teacher and calm practical system builder: kind expressive brown eyes, gentle confident smile, rounded friendly proportions, natural medium-brown fur with a cream muzzle and visible beaver incisors. His broad flat textured beaver tail must be clearly recognizable beside/behind the body. Wear a blue teacher's jacket over a simple light shirt and understated trousers. Hold a tablet comfortably at chest/waist height, with a few abstract log lines and tiny test-status indicators on its screen, no readable text. Render as a polished softly hand-painted editorial/cartoon character with delicate visible brush texture, gently modeled volume, warm soft light, tasteful detailed fur and fabric, and a friendly professor feeling similar to a storybook elephant professor. This is an original BEAVER character, not an elephant, not a copy of another character. Full body from ears to feet, complete tail, three-quarter standing pose, centered with generous transparent breathing room around every extremity. Single isolated character on a genuinely transparent background with alpha, no scene, no props other than the tablet, no pedestal, no baked checkerboard, no text, logos, signature or watermark. The silhouette, tail and warm face should remain legible as a small website character card.
```
